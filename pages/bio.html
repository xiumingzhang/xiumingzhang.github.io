Biography

Xiuming Zhang is currently a Principal Research Scientist and Senior Manager in the Spatial Intelligence Lab at NVIDIA, working on world (action) models. Prior to joining NVIDIA, he shipped Full Self-Driving (FSD) improvements to both customer and Robotaxi fleets as part of the Tesla Autopilot team and worked on computational photography at Adobe NextCam. In 2021, Zhang earned his Ph.D. from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), advised by William T. Freeman at MIT's Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL). His doctoral research focused on computer vision and computer graphics. At MIT, he was also mentored by Jonathan T. Barron and Yun-Ta Tsai during internships at Google Research. Zhang's doctoral dissertation Shape, Reflectance, and Illumination From Appearance explores inverse rendering and its applications, including 3D shape reconstruction and free-viewpoint relighting. Zhang completed his undergraduate studies at the National University of Singapore (NUS), where he conducted neuroscience research with B. T. Thomas Yeo. Zhang received a Snap Research Fellowship in 2019. His research has been exhibited at the MIT Museum and covered by outlets including the BBC, Forbes, Yahoo!, and Popular Mechanics.
